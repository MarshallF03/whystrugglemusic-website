Bradley McKnight is a totally blind singer-songwriter, music producer and rapper. He began his music journey by uploading cover songs to YouTube from his grandad's old tool shed, which he converted into a studio.

Bradley began releasing his own music in 2022, working with other producers, and took up music production himself in 2024. His music is available on all the major streaming platforms and has been played on local and national radio, including BBC Music Introducing in the East Midlands. He has also been featured in his local newspaper.

Bradley was also part of Able Orchestra, the UK's first disability-led orchestra, funded by Arts Council England and run by Inspire Youth Arts.

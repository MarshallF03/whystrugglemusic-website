Huskinson are a Nottinghamshire-based alt rock act who came together around lockdown to record a set of anthems that punch in the gut but still keep their melody and groove. The band features brothers Rob and Chris Huskinson on bass guitar and drums, with Marcus Shaw on vocals and Andy Dawson on guitar.

They recorded ten tracks, and six of them make up their first EP, Versus. The tracks are classic, timeless alt rock and show off great musicianship throughout. Look out for regular releases from the band through 2026 and 2027.

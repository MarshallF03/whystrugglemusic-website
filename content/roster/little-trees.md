Little Trees are a Nottinghamshire band that deliver a special brand of pop/rock that comes from the heart. The songs bounce and are instantly recognisable to a new audience. The band represent neurodivergent and disabled communities, and their songs reflect their own experiences in life. Full of joy and meaning, Little Trees are fresh and have an enthusiasm that defies the sceptics.

The band formed in 2024. They played OneFest in Mansfield in 2025 under their old name, Goflo, settled on Little Trees later that year, and came back to OneFest in 2026 under the new name. Since then they have played Lacewalks at Arc Space in Nottingham and released their first two singles, Stronger and So Now You Want Me.

Both singles got BBC Introducing airplay at the very first attempt. Dean Jackson played Stronger on BBC Introducing East Midlands on 12 September 2026, and So Now You Want Me on 15 October 2026, each the first time the band submitted it.

Little Trees have had support from Unanima Theatre Company as part of their Making a Musician project.

## One-to-one artist and band support through Why Struggle Records

So you've learned how to release your music, or you're just scratching the surface of the business, and you're starting to see the mounting paperwork you have to complete before each release. That includes registering your songs with a royalty collection agency, such as PRS for Music in the UK or your own country's performance rights organisation (PRO).

You're about creativity, not logistics, and Why Struggle Records can hold your hand through this entire process. The label can help you register, collect and monetise your music correctly, so you are not leaving money uncollected out there, or in "the black box", as many industry professionals call it.

The label can sit down with you on a video call and help you:

- upload your music to a distributor;
- register with your PRO;
- and with everything in between.

The label can also manage your content as an artist or band on your chosen social media accounts and platforms, saving your time and energy for what we all love: the music.

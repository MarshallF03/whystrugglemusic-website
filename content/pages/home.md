## Why Struggle is the solution to a problem

Why Struggle Music aims to solve problems for artists, producers and anyone who wants to learn the business side of the music industry. Why Struggle Music also offers tools and resources to break down the barriers that disabled musicians face, such as adaptations that make audio software usable with a screen reader for blind and visually impaired musicians. Why Struggle Music aims to do this while also offering support, guidance and resources to everyone who is interested in taking this music thing seriously.

Why Struggle Records aims to give artists the support they need to start their music journey, by offering advice, tools and resources that will give them the helping hand they have been waiting for all along.

## What you'll find here

Why Struggle Music is run by Bradley McKnight, a totally blind singer-songwriter, producer and rapper from Nottinghamshire. It brings together three things:

- **Why Struggle Records**, our record label and its roster of artists.
- **Music business hub**: plain-English guides to distribution, royalties, publishing, copyright and more.
- **Developing accessibility in music**: help, tools and links for blind and visually impaired musicians.

Everything else will follow as and when it is ready.

### Black Salt Audio plugin presets

- Native AU presets for Logic Pro, for every Black Salt Audio (BSA) plugin except BSA Mixbus.
- TFX presets for Pro Tools, including BSA Mixbus. BSA Mixbus presets are for Pro Tools only.

### GetGood Drums presets

- Native AU presets for GetGood Drums (GGD) Modern and Massive 2. These are for Logic Pro only for now.

### Multi-out templates

- **Spectre Digital Extinction Level Event**, for Logic Pro and Pro Tools:
    - a template with every available output;
    - Marshall Fairbrother's own template, built around Marshall's current tracking kit: kick drum, snare, two rack toms, a floor tom, two crashes, hi-hat and ride. It has no splash or china outputs.
- **GetGood Drums Modern and Massive 2**, for Logic Pro only for now.

We are not affiliated with Spectre Digital.

### Rock and metal tracking templates

Marshall's own rock and metal tracking templates for Logic Pro and Pro Tools, inspired by Jordan Valeriote's mixing templates. Each one will clearly list the plugins and sample libraries you need to use it.

### Installing

Each set of AU and TFX presets will come with an installer that puts the presets in the right preset folder for you. There will also be instructions for installing them by hand.

## Useful resources

- [OSARA](https://osara.reaperaccessibility.com/): a free extension that makes the REAPER digital audio workstation accessible with a screen reader.
- [Reaper Accessibility](https://reaperaccessibility.com/): a community wiki with guides, tutorials and a mailing list for blind REAPER users.
- [Drake Music](https://www.drakemusic.org/): a national charity working in music, disability and technology.
- [Attitude is Everything](https://www.attitudeiseverything.org.uk/): improving deaf and disabled people's access to live music.
- [RNIB](https://www.rnib.org.uk/): the Royal National Institute of Blind People, with advice on technology, work and everyday life with sight loss.
